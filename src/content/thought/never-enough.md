pubDate: 2026-06-15

draft: true
---

I realized something recently. When I look at friends around my age who are doing well, it's easy to assume they're going to be fine. I mean simple things: decent skills at work, being good with people, everyday competence. If someone is great socially but lacks technical work skills, I still assume they'll find a way to make those social skills work for them.

When it comes to myself, it's the exact opposite. No matter what I've worked for or put effort into, it feels like it's never enough. I refuse to acknowledge any leverage or advantage I actually have. Why the fuck am I so hard on myself?

Part of it is that as I get older, responsibilities keep piling up, and there's only one real path: grow up and face them. Then I see people online around my age who are so much better than me at what I do, and it gets in my head. It turns everything into a competition that never ends.

Maybe it's natural to feel this way when you feel responsible for where your life is heading. It might just be a phase I'm working through, and hopefully it pushes me in the right direction.

A reminder I need to keep coming back to:

> The only person you should try to be better than is the person you were yesterday.
